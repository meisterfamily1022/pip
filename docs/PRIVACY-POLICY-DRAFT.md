# Pip Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: [DATE]
Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

Pip V1 stores toy information, toy photos, locations, settings, and play history locally on the device. Pip V1 does not require an account, does not upload toy photos, does not use cloud backup, does not use advertising, does not use analytics, and does not send toy data to external AI services.

Parents control setup, the parent PIN, toy records, and settings. Children can use Child Mode to choose toys and complete cleanup with parent-controlled access back to Parent Mode.

Deleting Pip may delete locally stored toy data and photos. Pip V1 does not include server-side recovery.

This draft must be reviewed and completed by the app owner before publication.
# Analytics implementation update (2026-08-11)

Optional product analytics is off by default and available only to a signed-in adult. Guest and local-only use sends no telemetry. The collected categories, 13-month raw-event retention, parent opt-out/deletion controls, coarse optional geography, and prohibited child-identifying/precise-location fields are defined in `PIP_ANALYTICS_OPERATIONS.md`. App Store disclosures and legal review remain deployment tasks; this draft does not claim they are complete.
