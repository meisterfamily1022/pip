# PlayMap Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: [DATE]
Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

PlayMap V1 stores toy information, toy photos, locations, settings, and play history locally on the device. PlayMap V1 does not require an account, does not upload toy photos, does not use cloud backup, does not use advertising, does not use analytics, and does not send toy data to external AI services.

Parents control setup, the parent PIN, toy records, and settings. Children can use Child Mode to choose toys and complete cleanup with parent-controlled access back to Parent Mode.

Deleting PlayMap may delete locally stored toy data and photos. PlayMap V1 does not include server-side recovery.

This draft must be reviewed and completed by the app owner before publication.
